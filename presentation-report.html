EquipTrust

Institutional Technical Resource, Equipment & Space Sharing Platform

Problem Statement Unlock Underused Equipment and Spaces
Team Size 1 Member (Solo Builder)
Participant Name Ansh
Assigned Role Full-Stack Engineer & Architect

1. Problem Understanding & Selected Use Case

Universities, research laboratories, and makerspaces frequently possess millions of dollars worth of specialized machinery, testing devices, and cleanroom facilities (such as \$150k Scanning Electron Microscopes, 5-Axis CNC Mills, and Class 100 clean benches). Departmental audits show that these high-value tools sit idle 55% to 70% of evenings, weekends, and semester breaks. At the same time, nearby student researchers, engineering clubs (e.g. Formula Student, Robotics), and early-stage creators cannot afford commercial rates.

Traditional booking systems fail in this domain because they ignore safety prerequisites, chain of custody, before-and-after condition accountability, and anti-monopolization. Operating restricted equipment without training presents severe injury risks and liability; likewise, without pre-and-post inspection records, institutions cannot establish accountability for tooling damage.

2. Complete 10-Step Product Workflow

1. Resource Listing: Lab managers publish equipment with operating limits, weekly quotas, and required safety certifications (e.g. CNC Level 1, Bio/Radiation L2).
2. Discovery & Pre-Flight Eligibility: Users filter equipment with an "Eligible For Me" toggle that cross-checks user credentials against equipment prerequisites in real time.
3. Booking Request Submission: Users declare project purpose, materials, and requested duration with a safety pledge.
4. Automated System Verification: Real-time checks ensure: (a) certification validity, (b) weekly rolling quota allowances, and (c) scheduling conflicts. Overlapping requests are transparently placed in an explainable waitlist.
5. Manager Review: Lab managers review competing applicants side-by-side with explainable Fair Priority Scores.
6. Allocation & Status Lock: One-click Approve, Reject (with required written reason), or Waitlist.
7. Digital Check-In: Pre-operational station inspection, emergency stop verification, initial hour meter logging, and baseline condition photo.
8. Digital Return & Handover: Post-operation cleanliness verification, consumables logging, final meter reading, and return photo.
9. Damage & Incident Resolution: Tool fractures or delays trigger incident tickets with severity levels and automatic maintenance status locks until owner resolution.
10. Tamper-Evident Audit Ledger: Immutable chronological ledger documenting every action, actor, timestamp, and condition report.

3. Fair Allocation & Anti-Monopolization Mechanics

  • Rolling Weekly Quota Limits: Student researchers are hard-capped at 8.0 peak hours/week across shared tools to prevent end-of-semester reservation hoarding.
  • Explainable Priority Scoring: Contested slots are scored mathematically: Priority Score = Base(100) - 5*(RecentHoursUsed) + 15*(FirstTimeBonus) + 10*(CurricularDeadline)
  • Transparent Waitlist Escalation: Automated cascading promotion if confirmed reservations are cancelled >4 hours prior.
